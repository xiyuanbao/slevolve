    Reproducing the published results¶

    This page states which run of the benchmark cases corresponds to which result of the paper and what it should give.

    Case 1: Antarctic forward run¶

    Expected output

    • RUN_case1/OUTPUT_SLM/tgrid550.npy (Fortran) or OUT_case1_py/tgrid550.npy (Python).
    • Against the archived run: 2.05 m area-weighted RMS on a 12.3 m signal (0.024 m with the shelf-removal initialization mimicked); see Benchmark provenance and paper Sect. 4.4.

    Reproduction of the archived output (paper Sect. 4.4). With the total ice, SLEvolve reproduces the archived 550-yr topography to 2.05 m area-weighted RMS on a 12.3 m signal. The residual is the step-1 shelf removal: the archived run let the removed shelves enter the elastic and mass-conservation terms (and the rotational feedback) at step 1 but not the viscous memory. With that initialization mimicked, the agreement is 0.024 m RMS. The legacy code and SLEvolve agree with each other to machine precision (< 1e-11 m) in every configuration.

    Case 2: two glacial cycles¶

    Scope in the paper. One pass (the first outer iteration, from the present topography) is timed.

    Case 3: ICE-NADI-1¶

    Outer iteration. The complete ice age calculation is run with the outer iteration on the initial topography; the legacy accuracy is the misfit after the three passes from the present topography used by Kendall et al. (2005).

    Reference values. The misfit per outer pass of the reference run is in reference/case3.json (misfit_rms, misfit_max; Verifying the installation).

    Exactness and timing¶

    The exactness criteria against the legacy solver (paper Sect. 2.8) are in Release notes; the wall times of the reference runs are on Running the benchmark cases.
